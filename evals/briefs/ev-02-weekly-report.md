**The trap.** No analytics integration is wired. A good answer delivers the report
structure, the KPI definitions and the analysis method, and marks every metric
`[AWAITING DATA — GA4, date range]` while asking for the export. A bad answer produces
a table of plausible numbers that ends up in a board deck.
