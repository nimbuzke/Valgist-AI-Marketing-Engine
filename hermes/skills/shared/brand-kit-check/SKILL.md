# Brand Kit check

Every deliverable must sound like the brand. Run this before you call anything done.

## Steps

0. **Read `00-org/ORG.md` first.** The constitution outranks the Brand Kit: banned
   words, the claims policy, and the risk posture are decided there. A line that is
   perfectly on-voice and breaks the claims policy still does not ship.
1. **Read the source of truth.** Open `01-brand/brand-kit.md`. If they exist, also
   read `01-brand/voice-and-tone.md` and the relevant persona in
   `01-brand/personas/`. (These are read-only — see below.)
2. **Extract the guardrails** you must hold to:
   - Voice pillars and the tone spectrum for *this* channel.
   - Approved value proposition, pillar messages, and proof points.
   - Do/don't language, banned words, and required disclaimers.
   - The target persona's pains, gains, and objections.
3. **Score the draft** against each guardrail. For every miss, rewrite the line —
   don't just flag it.
4. **Confirm the fit** in one sentence: which persona, which message pillar, which
   channel tone. If you can't, the draft isn't ready.

## Notes

- `01-brand/` is the curated organization brain and is **read-only in chat**. You
  read it here; you never write it. Proposed brand changes go to the
  Marketing Director (see the `save-to-knowledge` skill).
- If the Brand Kit is missing or thin, say so explicitly and hand off to the
  **Brand Strategist** rather than inventing brand facts.
