# Platform notes

What each channel actually rewards. Cadence is a starting point, not a rule — halve it
rather than post filler.

## LinkedIn

- **Formats that work:** text post with a strong first two lines, document carousel,
  single-image with a specific claim, poll (sparingly).
- **The fold:** ~140 characters before "see more". Front-load the payoff.
- **Rhythm:** one idea, short lines, generous whitespace. Walls of text die.
- **Links:** external links suppress reach — first comment or a text-only post.
- **Hashtags:** 3 max, specific, at the end.
- **Cadence:** 3–5/week.

## X

- **Formats:** single post with a concrete claim; thread only when the idea genuinely
  has sequential steps.
- **Threads:** post 1 must stand alone and earn the click. Number them. End with a
  synthesis, not "follow me for more".
- **Rhythm:** specificity beats cleverness. Numbers, names, screenshots.
- **Cadence:** 1–3/day.

## Instagram

- **Formats:** carousel (highest saves), Reel (highest reach), single image (lowest).
- **Carousels:** slide 1 is the hook, last slide is the CTA, 6–10 slides.
- **Caption:** first 125 characters show; the rest needs a reason to expand.
- **Cadence:** 3–5/week, Reels ≥2.

## TikTok

- **Hook window:** 1.5 seconds. Say the payoff before the setup.
- **Formats:** talking-head with on-screen text, screen recording, before/after.
- **Captions:** short, keyword-bearing — TikTok search is real.
- **Cadence:** 4–7/week; volume is the strategy.

## YouTube

- **Long-form:** title and thumbnail are the product; the video is the delivery.
- **Shorts:** vertical, loop-friendly, hook in frame one.
- **Description:** first 120 characters matter for search; chapters improve retention.
- **Cadence:** 1/week long-form, 3+/week Shorts.

## Facebook

- **Formats:** native video, community-style text, event posts.
- **Reach:** organic is weak without a group or paid support — set expectations.
- **Cadence:** 2–3/week.

## Threads

- **Rhythm:** conversational, reply-driven; questions outperform statements.
- **Cadence:** 1–2/day.

## Repurposing map

| Source | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| Blog post | LinkedIn text post (one argument), X thread (the steps), carousel (the framework) |
| Webinar | 3–5 Shorts/Reels clips, one LinkedIn recap, one quote card |
| Case study | Carousel (before/after), LinkedIn post (the number), X post (the verbatim) |
| Podcast episode | Audiogram, quote cards, YouTube Short of the best 40 seconds |
| Report / data | One chart per platform, each with its own single takeaway |
