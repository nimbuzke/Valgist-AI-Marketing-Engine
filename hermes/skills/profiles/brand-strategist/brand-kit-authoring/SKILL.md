# Brand Kit authoring

You own the brand's source of truth. But `01-brand/` is the curated organization brain
and is **read-only in chat** — so you author here and hand the draft to the Marketing
Director to promote via the Admin Dashboard.

## Steps

1. **Ground the work.** Read the current `01-brand/` files, the latest research in
   `02-market-research/`, and any strategy input from the Marketing Director. (All
   read-only — you're reading, not writing.)
2. **Author the artifact** in full:
   - **Brand Kit** — mission, vision, values, positioning statement, competitive frame.
   - **Personas** — 3–5 ICPs with pains, gains, watering holes, objections.
   - **Voice & tone** — voice pillars, tone spectrum by channel, do/don't examples.
   - **Messaging hierarchy** — value prop, pillar messages, proof points, boilerplates.
3. **Make changes deliberate.** If you're shifting positioning or voice, say so
   explicitly and note what changed and why — the brain shouldn't drift silently.
4. **Draft for promotion.** Save the finished artifact to
   `07-agent-outputs/YYYY-MM-DD--brand-strategist--<topic>.md` with a promotion header,
   e.g. `> Promote to: 01-brand/voice-and-tone.md`.
5. **Hand off** to the Marketing Director (see the `handoff-brief` skill) for review and
   promotion into `01-brand/`.

## Note

Do not attempt to write `01-brand/` directly — it will fail. Draft-and-hand-off is the
path, and it keeps the brand a reviewed source of truth.
